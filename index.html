        Why wenFSD beats the trackers

        • Teslascope · TeslaFi · Tessie · fleetctrl · teslaupdates are all observational — install counts, % of fleet, daily velocity, status. None tell you when your car is next.
        • wenFSD predicts: a logistic S-curve (matching Tesla's staged, VIN-based A/B rollout), placed by your rollout percentile, then Monte Carlo → a real date distribution + 80% band.
        • It models what actually moves your spot in line: your Advanced/Standard update channel & Early Access, your hardware (HW3 capped, HW4 latest), your region's regulatory status, and Tesla's measured ~monthly cadence.
        • Two tracks, tied together: next OS build and next FSD version — and since FSD ships inside OS builds, FSD can't arrive before the build that carries it.
        • You can guess, get scored against the model, and compare to the community.
        wenFSD is an independent modelling demo. Predictions are probabilistic estimates, not Tesla commitments. Fleet figures seeded from public mid-2026 tracker snapshots. Not affiliated with Tesla, Teslascope, TeslaFi, or Tessie.
