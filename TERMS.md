# wenFSD — Terms of Use

_Last updated: 2026-06-21. Plain-language terms for an independent project, not legal advice._

## What wenFSD is

wenFSD is an independent tool that **estimates** when a Tesla vehicle may receive its next
software or FSD update. It is **not** affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Tesla, Inc.

## Predictions are estimates

All dates, windows, and probabilities are **statistical estimates** produced by a model. They
are **not** commitments by Tesla or by us, and they may be wrong. Tesla controls its own
rollout and can change, pause, or skip it at any time. Do not make decisions (purchases,
travel, etc.) in reliance on a wenFSD prediction.

Confidence bands shown are **modelled**, not empirically back-tested against real per-car
outcomes. Treat them accordingly.

## Sample vs live data

Where the interface shows **"sample data,"** the figures are illustrative and not live counts.
Live figures require a connected backend and/or your linked vehicle.

## Acceptable use
